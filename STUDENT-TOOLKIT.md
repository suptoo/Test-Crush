# Student Toolkit - Complete Access

## Overview
All teacher productivity tools are now available to students! Students get full access to 12+ powerful tools including the new Focus Studio, whiteboard, calculators, converters, and more.

## 📚 **All Available Tools**

### 🧠 **Focus & Concentration**
1. **Focus Studio** (NEW!)
   - Brain.fm-style concentration music
   - Binaural beats (5 modes)
   - 8 ambient soundscapes
   - Smart timer with progress tracking
   - Path: `/student/focus`

### 🛠️ **Productivity Tools**
2. **Whiteboard**
   - Digital canvas for drawing and diagrams
   - Multiple colors and brush sizes
   - Shapes: rectangle, circle, line
   - Undo/redo functionality
   - Export as PNG
   - Path: `/student/tools/whiteboard`

3. **Grade Calculator**
   - Calculate GPA (weighted and unweighted)
   - Letter grade conversion
   - Multiple grading scales
   - CSV export for records
   - Path: `/student/tools/grade-calculator`

4. **Study Timer**
   - Pomodoro technique support
   - Presets: 25, 45, 60 minutes
   - Custom duration
   - Audio alerts
   - Visual progress bar
   - Path: `/student/tools/timer`

5. **Assignment Checker**
   - Readability score (Flesch-Kincaid)
   - Grammar analysis
   - Word count and statistics
   - Quality recommendations
   - Export results
   - Path: `/student/tools/assignment-checker`

6. **Study Planner**
   - Weekly schedule planning
   - Learning objectives tracking
   - Materials and homework lists
   - localStorage persistence
   - Export to text
   - Path: `/student/tools/lesson-planner`

### 🔄 **File Converters**
7. **Image Converter**
   - Convert PNG, JPG, WebP
   - Batch processing
   - Image compression
   - Resize options
   - ZIP download
   - Path: `/student/tools/batch-converter`

8. **PDF to PPTX**
   - Convert PDF to PowerPoint
   - Browser-based processing
   - No upload to servers
   - Path: `/student/tools/pdf-to-pptx`

9. **PPTX to PDF**
   - Convert PowerPoint to PDF
   - Maintain formatting
   - Privacy-focused
   - Path: `/student/tools/pptx-to-pdf`

10. **Word to PPTX**
    - Convert Word docs to presentations
    - Automatic slide generation
    - Path: `/student/tools/word-to-pptx`

### 🎨 **Creative Tools**
11. **QR Code Generator**
    - Generate QR codes for any text/URL
    - Adjustable size
    - Download as PNG
    - Perfect for sharing study links
    - Path: `/student/tools/qr-generator`

12. **Text to Speech**
    - Convert text to natural speech
    - Multiple voices
    - Speed and pitch control
    - Study presets
    - Listen to textbooks or notes
    - Path: `/student/tools/text-to-speech`
